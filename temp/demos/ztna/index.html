Key ZTNA Concepts

No Network Access
NPA grants access to a specific application, not to the network segment. Even if the user's device is compromised, the attacker cannot laterally move across the network.
Identity-Aware
Every request is authenticated against your IdP (Azure AD, Okta, etc.). Access policy can be based on user group, device posture, location, and time — not just IP address.
No Inbound Ports
The NPA Publisher initiates outbound connections only. Your private app has zero exposed attack surface to the internet — eliminating the VPN appliance as an attack vector.
