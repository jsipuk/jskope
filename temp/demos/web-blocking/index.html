What You'll See

Block Page
A full-page Netskope block notification. Includes the policy category, reason, and your organisation's support contact. No pass-through option.
Coaching Notification
An inline banner or interstitial that explains why the site is flagged. User may be given an option to proceed with a justification, or the session is logged.
Allow with Logging
The site loads normally but Netskope logs the visit in the audit trail. Accessible in the Netskope tenant UI under Web Activity.
